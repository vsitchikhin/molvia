## packages/model

- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-text.ts` — `parseReceiptText`, `mergeParts`, `bestReading`, each with the country's card first (MOL-247): a receipt's text as Tesseract read it into lines with figures, every word and swap from the card (`lexiconOf`) — the till's two layouts and the class code «Դաս. 56.10» as a third reading (MOL-226), a section «Բաժին» with no items read as its sum and head (`departmentReceipt`, MOL-227), OCR's digit swaps undone by the line's arithmetic and the printed total, a sign read as a digit as the last resort (MOL-246), parts joined at the till's articles; the port of MOL-114's prototype (MOL-125).
- `packages/model/tests/entities/` — Fixtures `receipt-text.am-*.json`: four of the owner's receipts as read, the item rows and the total only under a made-up header, with what the prototype gave (В-7); and KFC's two prints, read by the class code (MOL-226).
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-card.ts` — A country's card, one record per country (MOL-247): its currency, the language of its names, and for a country read off a photo Tesseract's languages, the formats of numbers the parse reads, OCR's digit swaps and the model's (MOL-290) and letters read for digits, and the words the parse finds a total, the payment, the tax, the end of the list and the head by.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt.ts` — A receipt's statuses and why one failed, its limits (four parts, the size of a part, 28 days), the countries read, `needsReshoot` (В-4) and the lines in the domain's money and quantities.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-sum.ts` — The arithmetic of a receipt under review, one on the phone and the server (MOL-124 В-6): a line adds up with its product rounded to the receipt's digits (П-2), what a line is recorded at (В-5), «Строки», the difference with the total and its suspect line, a price one confused digit off the memory's (В-1); `linesTotalOf`, what the lines come to by a total that sends its discount to the card, and `receiptPaidOf`, what left the card (MOL-289).
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-verify.ts` — `verifiedReading`: what of a reading goes to the review as read (MOL-255) — a line as printed with a quantity and a sum above zero, one piece whose price is its sum — a model's within the till's rounding (`byModel`, MOL-272) — only on a receipt proven whole, the price only where the arithmetic holds; the rest emptied, `balanced` as «proven whole»; `explainedReading` — the same, with why each line was not shown as read and why the receipt is not whole (MOL-309, `RECEIPT_LINE_REASONS`, `RECEIPT_REASONS`).
- `packages/model/src/contracts/receipt-miss.ts` — The developer's notice of a receipt the model read not whole (MOL-309): the id's start, the country, the lines found and shown, the receipt's reason and the lines' by kind, until when it is kept; and the count held back past the hour's budget.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-model.ts` — a model's answer (MOL-272): `modelReceiptSchema` the reader asks for and parses back, `receiptTextOfModel` into the parse's `ReceiptText` — figures as printed, a line as printed where its own arithmetic holds, the layout by a discount printed, the total by the card's words (MOL-244), never «since the start of the year» (MOL-288), a card's discount sent to the card and a bonus spent (MOL-289), a line put right by the font's 5↔6 only with the whole receipt (MOL-290, `repairTries` for the tests), a figure in any spelling the model writes it and `unparsedFigures` for the log (MOL-294), two fiscal numbers the key as a pair (MOL-293), the total's repair first and on its own, then the shop's memory and the repair again over what it decided (MOL-291); `modelHeadRows` for the city.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-remembered.ts` — the shop's memory as a voice in a line's digits (MOL-291): `rememberedLines` — a price that confirms a line as printed, puts right one that does not hold with its sum as read — the other figures by the swaps (the model's) or as printed (Tesseract's), never where the reader's swaps explain it at another price (`withoutMemory`) — doubts one as printed 5↔6 off the person's own price; a sum changed and a doubt the total's to take, a total missed refuting what it put right; `rememberedReading` for Tesseract after the parse chose; `cardRate`; ceilings and `rememberedTries` for the tests.
- `packages/model/src/entities/model-spend.ts` — What reading a receipt by a model costs (MOL-272): the model and its price in the code, a call at its `usage` in micro-dollars, its worst before it goes, the steps of the month's cap the owner hears of, the month in UTC.
- `packages/model/tests/entities/model-spend.test.ts` — Unit test of the model's price: a call at its usage and the cache, the worst bound by pixels and bytes, the steps at half and nine tenths exactly, the month in UTC.
- `packages/model/src/contracts/model-spend.ts` — The owner's notice of what the model has cost this month (MOL-272): the month, the step — 50, 90, the stop — what was spent and the cap.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-line-kind.ts` — What a line of an Armenian till is (MOL-293): `receiptLineParts` — its kind, the head word before the brand, the brand in «…», the fat and the package; `seedNameFits` — whether a seed's till name says its kind, inflected or with a letter lost, and both of two things side by side; `lineFitter` — whether any other item may stand on it, by its kind and brand and nothing it does not print, its words by their Russian forms; `newLineName` — a new item's name in full, the brand in Russian letters (`armenianInRussian`), and whether a word of it is spelt in letters.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-pack.ts` — The package a line's name prints (MOL-264): `receiptPack` — what one piece of the till holds, a weight, a volume or a pack of pieces, an Armenian till's words or a Serbian journal's, none where it is not read for sure (a 6 off a photo in a size or a count, a unit not glued on a photo, a range, a fraction, another count beside it, a Latin «h» beside a size, a thickness, two sizes, past what a package holds); `packedQuantity` — the purchase, the till's pieces times it.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-match.ts` — `createLineMatcher`: a receipt line to a catalogue item by the item's names in the till's language, the customs heading ruling out what cannot be, a variety by the fat printed, the heading alone as a far match, a dish of a class of services as a far one (MOL-226); `headingRulesOut` for an item the search found (MOL-255); the gloss by the dictionary of till words — the port of MOL-114's `dict-match.mjs` (MOL-126); a name or a heading only for an item the line lets stand (MOL-293).
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-link.ts` — `serbianReceiptLink`: a Serbian receipt's link from its QR code read with no network (MOL-232) — the tax office's host, `vl` of a receipt's size with its MD5 holding, a sale only (a refund, a copy, a pro forma refused) — and the total, the moment and the number signed in it; nothing of the buyer.
- `packages/model/src/entities/receipt-journal.ts` — `serbianJournal`: the lines of a Serbian receipt from the tax office's 40-column journal, Serbia's card ported from MOL-223 — the unit by the name's last unit word; `serbianItemName`, the name a line gives an item; `serbianShopOf`, the premises' code and the shop's name; `serbianCityOf`.
- `packages/model/src/support/md5.ts` — `md5Hex`: MD5 of bytes in the domain, for the signature at the end of a Serbian receipt's link — no `node:crypto` there, and Web Crypto has none.
- `packages/model/tests/entities/serbian-receipt.ts` — Test data: a Serbian receipt's link built and signed here and a journal under a head that names nobody (the repository is public), shared with the backend's and end-to-end tests.
- `packages/model/src/contracts/receipt.ts` — «Отправить чек» and a receipt on the wire: the body named by the phone, the summary in «Покупки», the lines as read.

## backend · schema

The tables are in the skeleton's schema: `receipts`, `receipt_parts`, `receipt_lines`,
`receipt_line_images`, migration `0042_receipts`. Erasure takes them through `receipts`; the copy
carries receipts and their lines, never a photo. MOL-126 (`0045_receipt_lines_bound`) binds a line to
its item and purchase and adds the item as a node — `item_names` (names in the tills' languages) and
`item_hs` (customs headings), both the seed's — `receipts_trip_key` (one trip, one receipt), and `store_memory`, the shops' shared
memory: erasure leaves its words without an author, the copy carries the person's own. MOL-232
(`0058_receipt_links`) gives a receipt its `source` — a photo, or a Serbian receipt's link asked of the
tax office, with no parts — the next ask and the premises' code and name; the link while it is asked
is `receipt_links`, a table the nightly copy leaves out. MOL-289/290 (`0068_receipt_cashback_repaired`): a receipt's `cashback_minor` and `redeemed_minor`, a line's `repaired`.
