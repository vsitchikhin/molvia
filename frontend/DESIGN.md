# Design System: Molvia

> The token block above is generated from `frontend/src/styles/_tokens.scss` by `bin/design-md.mjs`
> and is normative: `make format` writes it, `make lint` refuses one out of date. Keys ending in
> `-dark` are the dark scheme's value of the same role. Rules marked **(target)** were decided on
> 01.10.2026 (MOL-118, `FIXES.md` of the design package) and are not yet in the app: draw them, do
> not copy the old frames that contradict them.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Shelf in Bad Light"**

Molvia answers one question for emigrants in Armenia: _what is worth buying, and where_. Every
screen is designed from one moment — a phone in one hand at a supermarket shelf, under shop lamps
at eight in the evening, the other hand holding a basket. Desktop is derived from that, never the
other way round.

So the system is warm, quiet and legible before it is anything else. The ground is a warm paper
(`sunken`), never cold white: under shop lighting a white sheet is the brightest object in the
aisle. The dark scheme is a real warm-ink scheme with its own values, read just as often — in the
evening, at home, counting money. Type is large (17 base), numbers line up in columns, colour never
carries meaning alone. There are no ads, no "recommended" places, no promoted rows: trust in the
verdict is the one asset that cannot be written off, and the visual system must never look like it
is selling something.

The personality is a calm neighbour who has already tried the cheese: plain words, a firm verdict,
no fuss. Not a fintech dashboard, not a coupon app.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm paper ground, cards on it; a real dark scheme, not an inversion.
- Terracotta means "you can press this" and nothing else.
- The verdict is carried by form (fill, outline, strike-through), colour only confirms it.
- Big type, tabular numbers, the currency sign after the number.
- One thumb: the main action sits in a bar above the tab bar.
- Quiet: no toasts, no spinners on buttons, no glass, no grabber on sheets.

## Colors

A warm, low-chroma ground with four semantic roles — accent, good, warn, bad — each with a base
(non-text), an ink (text) and a tint (fill) step, in both schemes.

### Primary

- **Terracotta** (`accent` #b2622d / dark #e8925a): rules, the active tab, focus rings, indicators. Non-text only in light (4.3:1).
- **Fired Terracotta** (`accent-solid` #8c491a): filled controls that carry text — the primary button (6.5:1 with `on-accent`).
- **Terracotta Ink** (`accent-ink` #8c491a / dark #f2ad7e): the accent at text size — ghost buttons, links, "Повторить" inside a section.
- **Peach Wash** (`accent-tint`): only under something pressable (the tinted button, the active combobox row).

### Semantic

- **Olive** (`good`, `good-ink`, `good-tint`): the "Брать" verdict, a reconciliation that came out even, a plus against the market.
- **Ochre** (`warn`, `warn-ink`, `warn-tint`): "Только если дёшево", stale data, attention.
- **Brick** (`bad`, `bad-ink`, `bad-tint`; dark — crimson, held off the terracotta): "Не брать", errors, a minus against the market, destructive words.

### Neutral

- **Warm Paper** (`sunken` #f3e8d6 / dark #14110f): the page ground.
- **Cream Card** (`surface` #fffaf2 / dark #201c19): cards, sheets, the bars.
- **Well** (`surface-2`): inputs, segmented tracks, inert pills, empty-state circles.
- **Seam** (`border`, `border-strong`): card edges, the secondary button's outline, the edge of a field — decoration only.
- **Graphic** (`graphic` #746258 / dark #857a6c): data that carries meaning without a colour of its own — 3:1 on `surface`.
- **Ink** (`text` #1f1c19) and **Faded Ink** (`text-muted` #6c6152): text; muted clears 4.5:1 on `surface`, `sunken` and `surface-2` — not on a tint, where a secondary line is `text`.

### Categories of spending

`cat-*` (13 presets, 8 for one's own categories). A category colour is a dot or a bar, never text,
and always stands beside its name.

### Named Rules

**The Terracotta Means Press Rule.** The accent marks what can be pressed, focused or is selected —
never data, never status, never decoration. A chart series is `text` or `graphic`, not accent
**(target)**. A note or hint sits on `surface-2`, not on `accent-tint` **(target)**.

**The Neighbouring Roles Rule.** Any two steps of different roles — accent (with `accent-solid`),
good, warn, bad, `graphic` — and every category against every step of a role stand at least 0.08
apart in OKLab, in both schemes; a mark and a category are 3:1 on `surface`, text 4.5:1 on its ground,
and a tint, a fill with no edge, stands 0.08 from `surface` — a notice in a sheet or a card. On the page
ground (`sunken`) the light good, warn and bad tints stand closer (0.071, 0.070, 0.040): a strip or a
state's circle there is told by its icon and word, not by the edge of its fill.
`frontend/src/styles/tokens.test.ts` holds every pair, with no list of exceptions: a value that fails
is changed, never excused (MOL-172). Categories among themselves are not held yet (MOL-218): today a
name beside the colour tells two close ones apart.

**The Graphic Is Data Rule.** Anything that carries meaning — an unselected bar, the "Остальные"
sector, the "no data" dash, the off track of a switch — is drawn in `graphic`, at least 3:1 on
`surface`. `border-strong` (1.8:1) is decoration only. The charts, the countdown of "Вернуть" and the
switch still draw `border-strong` until their own tasks **(target)**.

**The Colour Never Alone Rule.** Beside every colour stands a word, a form or a figure: a verdict has
its icon and word, a category its name, a plus or minus its sign and "лучше / хуже рынка".

**The Offline Is Never Red Rule.** No connection is calm (`good`) or stale (`warn`); red is only an
error that offers "Повторить".

## Typography

**Display Font:** Nunito 800 (with Onest, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Onest 400 / 600 / 700 (with system-ui)
**Dram sign:** a face of its own (`Dram`, U+058F only) placed first in both stacks, so `֏` sits on the
baseline of a price.

**Character:** a rounded, friendly display face for the answers of the screen — titles and money —
over a neutral, highly legible text face with real Cyrillic.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (Nunito 800, 34, 1.15, −0.01em): the large screen title; collapses to Headline in the bar.
- **Figure** (Nunito 800, 28): money that is the answer to the screen.
- **Title** (Nunito 800, 24): sheet titles, section titles.
- **Card figure** (Nunito 800, 20) and **Entry** (Nunito 800, 40 with a 32 sign): two amounts side by side on a card; the amount being typed.
- **Headline** (Onest 600, 17): a row's title, the collapsed bar title.
- **Body** (Onest 400, 17, 1.45): everything read.
- **Callout** (Onest 400, 15): a row's second line.
- **Label / Footnote** (Onest 600 / 400, 13): field labels, meta, unit prices.
- **Caption** (Onest 700, 11, caps, +0.06em): group captions only; tab labels 11/600 without caps.

### Named Rules

**The One Weight Per Role Rule.** Everything set in Nunito is 800 — `--weight-display`, always
together with the face through the `display-type` mixin; Stylelint allows the two nowhere else, and
the app ships Nunito at 800 only. Never 400 or 700 on a display role; a sentence in a figure's place
("нет данных") is set in Onest.

**The 17 Base Rule.** Body text is 17. Nothing below 13 holds a sentence: 11 is for caps captions,
tab labels and axis labels only **(target)** — explanations like "Цены нет намеренно…" move to 13.

**The Money In Columns Rule.** Every amount and quantity is `tabular-nums`; the currency sign follows
the number after a no-break space ("5 403 ֏"); whole amounts print without ",00". A converted amount
is an estimate: `≈`, smaller, `text-muted`, rounded to whole units, never in a unit price.

## Layout

A single column at 390 × 844 (frames are drawn 412 × 866 with the phone). The page scrolls, never an
inner container — a sheet is the only exception.

- **Page gutter:** 16 on every screen **(target: "Деньги" uses 32 today)**.
- **Spacing scale:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 — nothing off the scale. Inside a group 8, between groups 24 **(target: not 12 between everything)**.
- **Bars:** the top bar is one touch target (44) under the safe area; the large title collapses into it after 24 px of scroll. The tab bar is 74 plus the home indicator, five tabs: «Что брать · Покупки · Оценки · Деньги · Настройки», home is «Что брать».
- **Docked action:** a screen's main action is a full-width bar (`#docked`) above the tab bar — on «Покупки» and on every screen of «Деньги» **(target: no floating buttons)**. Nothing scrolls under it unseen: the list ends above it.
- **Above the control, nothing of the answer:** a strip, a refusal, a note go under the switch (month, period) that chooses the answer, never above it.
- **Thumb zone:** a state's action is pushed to the bottom of the free height; one primary per screen.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat and tonal: depth comes from ground → card → well (`sunken` → `surface` → `surface-2`),
with warm, shallow shadows — a phone held at 30 cm shows banding in anything deeper.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Rest** (`0 1px 2px rgb(63 46 26 / 10%)`): cards, the primary button.
- **Raised** (`0 4px 14px rgb(63 46 26 / 14%)`): a selected segment, a strip over content.
- **Sheet** (`0 -8px 32px rgb(46 33 18 / 22%)`): sheets rising from the bottom edge.

### Named Rules

**The No Glass Rule (target).** The top bar and the tab bar are opaque `surface` with a hairline
`border` — no translucency, no blur (today 88 % with a 12 px blur, and page text shows through).

## Shapes

Soft but not bubbly. Radii: 2 (`mark`, the corner of a bar, a legend key, a tick), 8 (`sm`, a
segment, a small well), 14 (`default`, fields, list cards,
the "Брать" card), 20 (`lg`, cards), 24 (`sheet`, the top corners of a sheet), pill (buttons, badges,
chips, search). Borders are a 1 px hairline; a verdict badge's outline is 1.5 px, because 1 px
disappears in bad light. Circles are a form (`50%`), not a size.

## Components

Native HTML first, then Reka UI; never a styled kit. Icons are MDI. Every touch target is at least
44; the sheet's main action is 52.

### Buttons

- **Shape:** pill, 44 high (52 as the main action of a sheet), 16 side padding, a 22 icon before the word.
- **Primary:** `accent-solid` fill, `on-accent` text 700, rest shadow. One per screen.
- **Secondary:** `surface` with a `border-strong` hairline, `text` 600.
- **Tinted (target, new):** `accent-tint` fill, `accent-ink` text — "Вернуть", "Записать разницу", "Добавить счёт". The old handoffs called this "secondary"; it is a variant of its own.
- **Ghost / Danger ghost:** no fill, `accent-ink` / `bad-ink` text. "Убрать" a category is ghost, not danger: it erases nothing **(target)**.
- **Icon:** a 44 circle on `surface-2`.
- **Inactive (target):** one look everywhere — solid `surface-2`, `text-muted`, still focusable (`aria-disabled`), and the words say why ("Выберите оценку"). No opacity.
- **Focus:** a 2 px `accent` ring, 2 px out.

### Selection (target)

One grammar: the selected thing gets a fill and a weight, not only a hue. The active tab has a filled
MDI icon and a 700 label; inactive tabs are outlined, 600. A selected segment is filled
`accent-solid` with `on-accent`. A selected chip has the `accent-tint` fill and an `accent` ring, and
its weight does not change on selection, so the row never reflows under the thumb.

### Cards / Lists

- **Card:** `surface`, radius 20, 16 padding, `border` hairline, rest shadow.
- **List card:** the same with no padding; rows divided by a hairline; focus drawn inside the row.
- **ListRow (target, new):** icon 24 · title 17/600 · meta 13 · tail (amount, button or a 20 chevron); 64 high.
- **SectionCaption (target, new):** caps 11/700, `text-muted`, 4 from the left, 8 above its card.
- **NavRow (target, new):** "icon · label · value · ›" — the entry into a nested screen.
- **OperationRow (target, one):** one row for spendings, an account's log and a reconciliation; a chevron on every pressable row or on none; amounts aligned in one column.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Field:** a 44 well on `surface-2`, radius 14, hairline `border-strong`; label above in 13/600 `text-muted`; focus turns the edge `accent` with a 2 px `accent-tint` halo; the error is a word from the registry under the field. Never `type="number"`.
- **SearchField (target, one):** a pill well on `surface-2`, a magnifier left, "Очистить" or the scanner right. One look for every search in the app.
- **Segmented control:** a `surface-2` track, segments 38 that answer the thumb over the full 44.

### Verdict badge (signature)

Three forms, readable without colour: **Брать** — filled `good`, ✓, word; **Только если дёшево** —
`warn` outline 1.5, ~; **Не брать** — `bad` outline 1.5, ✕, struck-through word. A pill with the
word, or a 22 dot (24 beside a group title). There is no price beside "Не брать нигде" — no number,
no dash: comparing a bad thing by price is exactly what the product refuses. Inside the "Брать"
group the card does not repeat the badge **(target)**.

### Screen states

Every screen has four — loading, empty, error, offline — drawn by two blocks only.

- **Skeleton:** bars in the geometry of the answer, uneven widths, a slow breath (still under reduced motion). **(target)** inside a `surface` card, the same shape as the answer (with the search field on «Что брать»), visible in light (today 1.06:1 on the ground).
- **State block:** a 38 circle with a 22 icon, a 17/600 title, a 13 text, one action at the bottom of the free height. The circle is `surface-2` for empty **(target: not terracotta — it is not pressable)**, `bad-tint` for error, `good`/`warn` for offline, `warn` for attention. The words tell the truth of the state ("Оценивать пока нечего — сначала запишите покупку").
- **StatusStrip (target, one):** a thin line under the header — cloud and "Без связи · показано сохранённое на 14:05" — once per screen, in that screen's words. "From memory" has two forms only: a state block when there is no answer, the strip when an old answer is shown.
- **Error:** one "Повторить" per screen, at one height; a section's error is an `accent-ink` "Повторить" inside the section. Never two filled primaries in a row.

### Sheet

A native dialog rising from the bottom: `surface`, radius 24 on top, at most 82 % of what is visible
above the keyboard. Closes by ×, the scrim, Esc, "back" and **a pull down** — no grabber is drawn.
A sheet over a sheet has only "‹" on the left and no ×; one scrim. **(target)** The main action is
pinned to the bottom edge over the content with the sheet shadow; the content scrolls under it.

### Navigation

The chevron's label is the parent's name, never "Назад"; the back control is 26. No gesture is
intercepted except the sheet's own pull down.

### Charts

Four kinds only: donut, bars, line, horizontal bars — no areas, stacks or second axis. Selection on
lift of the finger or as soon as it moves sideways; the selected bar is `accent`, the others
`graphic`. A donut's sectors and "Остальные" come from the server; the centre is not a live region.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** design every screen at 390 × 844 in both schemes, with all four states.
- **Do** put the main action in the docked bar above the tab bar.
- **Do** use only tokens: no hex, no off-scale spacing, no literal weight, size or radius, and no
  custom property that is defined nowhere — Stylelint refuses each of them.
- **Do** write every text as the person speaks — «Цена как на ценнике», not «Введите стоимость».
- **Do** show money in the person's own currency first, the conversion second with `≈`.
- **Do** draw the decided rules marked (target), not the old frames that contradict them.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use terracotta for data, status or decoration.
- **Don't** let colour be the only sign of anything — selection, verdict, plus or minus.
- **Don't** put a price, a dash or a "cheapest" badge beside "Не брать нигде"; don't name any superlative.
- **Don't** add ads, sponsored rows, store logos, photos or "recommended" places.
- **Don't** draw floating action buttons, toasts, spinners on buttons, glass, or a sheet grabber.
- **Don't** set a sentence below 13, or a display role in anything but Nunito 800.
- **Don't** put two filled primary buttons on one screen, or "Повторить" twice.
- **Don't** speak the developer's language on screen («локальная копия», «сервер считает»).
