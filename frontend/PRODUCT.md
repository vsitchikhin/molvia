# Product: Molvia · Молвия

**What it answers.** What is worth buying, and where, in an unfamiliar country. Not an expense
tracker — that niche is taken. Everyone counts money; nobody answers whether this cheese is edible.

**The rule that holds it together.** Cheap but bad is never recommended anywhere; good is looked up
at its lowest price.

**Who.** Russian-speaking emigrants in Armenia — Gyumri and Yerevan first. They earn in roubles and
spend in drams, read Armenian labels with difficulty, and do not yet know which shop has what.
Interface language: Russian first, English mirrored; Armenian names appear in data.

**Where and how.** A phone in one hand at a shelf, the other holding a basket, under shop lamps or
in the evening at home in the dark scheme. Short sessions: find a thing, see the verdict, record a
purchase, rate it the next day. Connection drops in the aisle — every screen works from memory and
says so calmly.

**The two streams.** A _verdict_ is rare — one per «item + place» — and it is the core. An _expense_
is frequent, an accounting layer under «Деньги»: spendings, accounts, exchanges against the market,
incomes, charts. They are separate screens with separate motivations.

**Tone.** A calm neighbour who has already tried it: plain spoken Russian, firm verdicts, no
exclamation marks, no jargon, no developer words. Honest about uncertainty («≈», «курс на 25 сент.»).

**Trust is the asset.** No ads, no paid placements, no "recommended" shops, no store logos or photos,
no superlatives from the server. Other people's prices and ratings appear only as aggregates of at
least three people. Expenses are always private.

**Anti-references.** Coupon and cashback apps (loud, selling); generic fintech dashboards (a stack of
identical cards, numbers without a question); cold white minimalism (glare under shop light).

**Releases.** 0.1 — the owner uses it daily. 0.2 — strangers fill the base (barcodes, receipts).
0.3 — they come back for other people's data. Features are cut by hypothesis, not by wish.
